- **(c) `metier`** (`MET-`, security) — **domain invariants never enforced**: finite resource assignable twice (uniqueness, transaction, lock absent) or exceeded by concurrent requests; amounts, quantities, discounts, owner supplied by the client and accepted as is; state transitions without checking the starting state; no throttling on authentication or costly operations, enumeration possible; personal data collected beyond what is necessary, authentication secrets without robust derivation, personal data in logs, no retention period or deletion; no usable trace after an incident. **The most profitable family and the least covered by tooling**: it requires reconstructing rules written nowhere.
