- **(c) `fuites`** (`LEAK-`) — leaks and confounders in evals: information crossing the reset through a channel other than the one measured; global state not reset between conditions; seed or budget (steps, tokens, precision) differing by condition; metric on a different subset; train/eval mode; silent truncation or `try/except`; memory write during recall where the protocol forbids it; control documented but absent from the script.
