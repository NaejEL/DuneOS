# SPEC-leg-10 — Broaden CI to several boards and profiles

## Context

Finding LEG-10 (major, M): `.github/workflows/ci.yml` hardcodes a single target. Lines 33 and 67:
`echo m5stack-cardputer > .duneos_board`. Line 71: `--profile cardputer-contest`.

The repository however holds 6 boards (`esp32c3-devkitc`, `esp32p4-devkitm`, `esp32s3-devkitc`,
`kincony-A16`, `lilygo-t-embed-cc1101`, `m5stack-cardputer`) and 7 profiles. Five boards out of six
and six profiles out of seven are never built, and no RISC-V target is compiled.

The project explicitly claims multi-board and multi-architecture support (CLAUDE.md documents the
procedure for adding an architecture and the three-condition `arch.cmake` guard pattern), but
nothing verifies that the promise holds: a change to `bspgen.py`, `board_config.h` or an
`arch.cmake` can break five boards without any job reporting it.

## Scope

Turn the `kernel-build` and `apps-build` jobs into matrix builds covering several boards, and extend
profile builds beyond `cardputer-contest` alone.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The kernel build job runs over a matrix covering at least three boards, including
   `m5stack-cardputer` and at least one board whose architecture is not `xtensa_esp32s3`.
2. For every matrix entry, `IDF_TARGET` is resolved from `boards/<board>/idf_target.txt` generated by
   `duneos-bspgen.py`, not hardcoded in the workflow.
3. Every matrix entry starts from a clean tree: the `sdkconfig` and `build/` directory of one board
   are never reused for another (the clean-on-board-change constraint is honoured).
4. The application build job builds at least two profiles, including `cardputer-contest`.
5. A build failure in a single matrix entry fails the workflow.
6. Any board present in `boards/` but deliberately excluded from the matrix is listed with the reason
   for its exclusion, either in the workflow or in the CI documentation — a board must never go
   unbuilt through mere oversight.
7. On the current commit, every retained matrix entry passes.

## Out of scope

Fixing boards that fail to build (each discovered failure yields its own finding and spec);
completing `arch/riscv32/` (LEG-24, not retained in this cycle); testing on real hardware; building
all 7 profiles.

## Risks

Broadening the matrix will likely reveal boards that do not build today. That is the point of the
spec, but it must not make it overflow: criterion 6 allows explicitly excluding a broken board with
documentation, and the out-of-scope section defers the fix to a dedicated spec. Mind build time and
CI quotas too: every matrix entry spins up a full ESP-IDF container.
